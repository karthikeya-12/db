# Q3 Business Review - Northwind Logistics

Revenue for the third quarter was 12.4 million euros, up 8 percent from the second quarter.
The Hamburg warehouse expansion finished two weeks ahead of schedule and added 4,000 pallet positions.
Customer churn fell to 2.1 percent after the introduction of the new tracking dashboard, which was led by Priya Raman.
The main risk identified for Q4 is a shortage of drivers in the Benelux region.

# Vendor Agreement: Helios Cold Storage

Helios Cold Storage provides refrigerated storage to Northwind Logistics under a contract signed on 14 March 2025.
The contract runs for 36 months and renews automatically unless either party gives 90 days' written notice.
Monthly fees are 18,500 euros. A late payment incurs interest of 1.5 percent per month.
The contact person at Helios Cold Storage is Tomas Berg.

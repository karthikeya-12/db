# Northwind Logistics Employee Handbook

## Time off
Full-time employees at Northwind Logistics receive 25 days of paid vacation per year. Unused vacation days can be carried over
into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Sick leave is separate and is not capped.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The Rotterdam head office keeps a hot-desk booking system for the other days.

## Expenses
Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Expenses above 500 euros need written approval from the
employee's department head before purchase.
